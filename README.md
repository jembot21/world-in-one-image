One image that sums up the world news of Wednesday 18 November 2026: a one-page newspaper, "The Daily Globe", drawn by an AI agent — the U.S. strikes Iran's Qeshm Island missile base after drone attacks on Bahrain and Kuwait, Ukraine strikes the Kerch Bridge for a second straight day, Xi opens APEC in Shenzhen ahead of a meeting with Trump and Putin, Nvidia posts a record $115.8bn quarter as Wall Street slides, Mary Peltola wins Alaska and gives Democrats 56 Senate seats, the U.S. loses its measles-free status, an Indonesian police general is fired in a presidential wiretap scandal, Congo's Ebola outbreak more than doubles, Cyclone Xavier hits the Cook Islands, and Voyager 1 nears one light-day from Earth. The program at main.ts copies entry.png to /output/entry.png.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
